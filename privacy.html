Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

The short version

  • What you type stays on your computer. reverse.ai reads your typing to spot text typed in the wrong keyboard. It keeps only the line you're typing (and, for one minute, the line before it), in memory. It never saves it, and sends it only if you click Report.
  • No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. We don't sell or share personal information.
  • One daily update check. Once a day the app asks our website whether a newer version exists. The request carries nothing about you or your typing, but like any web request it shows your IP address to the website's host. You can turn it off.
  • Reports only when you click. If reverse.ai gets something wrong, Report sends us those words and a few technical details, with no name or identifier. Nothing is sent unless you click it.

1. Who we are

We are responsible for the small amount of personal information described in this policy, for the purposes of Israel's Protection of Privacy Law, 5741-1981, and similar laws elsewhere.

2. How the app handles your typing

  • To work, the app needs the Accessibility permission (Mac) or a keyboard hook (Windows), which lets it see the keys you press in other apps.
  • It keeps only the line you're currently typing, in memory. When the line ends (you press Return, click or switch apps), it keeps that line in memory for one more minute, so its menu can offer to report it, and then forgets it. It doesn't keep lines of a single word, very long lines, or anything typed in a password field.
  • It never writes what you type to disk and never sends it over the internet, unless you click Report (section 4d). We never see it otherwise.
  • It ignores password fields, apps on its exclusion list (password managers, by default) and words that contain digits.
  • When you accept a fix, the app deletes the mistyped text and types the correction. It doesn't use your clipboard.
  • All language processing, including Smart mode's small AI model, runs on your computer.

3. What's saved on your computer

  • Your settings, such as sensitivity, Smart mode and excluded apps.
  • A diagnostic log, only if you turn it on (the app's menu → "Keep a diagnostic log"), for example to report a problem. It stays on for 24 hours and records events, counts and the name of the app you were typing in (for example, "bubble shown for 3 words in WhatsApp"), never the text. It's saved on your computer only (Mac: ~/Library/Logs/reverse.ai; Windows: %LOCALAPPDATA%\reverse.ai\log.txt), reaches us only if you choose to send it, and is deleted 7 days after it was last written.
  • Early test versions could save a short list of words you undid twice, so they wouldn't be fixed again. Current versions don't add to it, and the app's menu can clear it.

Uninstalling the app (and, on Windows, deleting its reverse.ai folders) removes this data.

4. What leaves your computer

a. The daily update check

Once a day, and when you choose "Check for updates now", the app downloads a tiny file from reverseai.vercel.app that holds the latest version number. The request contains no identifier, account or typing data. Our website host, Vercel Inc. (USA), receives your IP address, the time and basic technical data, as with any web request, and handles it under its own privacy policy. We don't use this to identify or track you. To turn it off: the app's menu → "Check for updates automatically".

b. Our website and downloads

When you visit our website or download the app, Vercel and GitHub Inc. (USA, which hosts the download files) receive your IP address and similar request data. The website loads fonts from Google Fonts (Google LLC), which also receives your IP address. The website remembers your language choice in your browser's local storage. It uses no cookies, analytics or advertising.

c. What you send us

If you email us or send us a log file, we use it only to answer you and fix problems, and we delete it once it's no longer needed (within 12 months at most), or sooner if you ask.

d. Reports you choose to send

When you click Report (in the suggestion bubble or in the app's menu), the app sends us: the words in question, what reverse.ai changed them to or would have, which keyboard languages were involved, why the change was suggested, the name of the app you were typing in, your reverse.ai and operating-system versions, your reverse.ai settings, and anything you write in the optional note. The first time, the app shows what's included; every report waits 4 seconds so you can undo it. A report contains no name, account, email address or device identifier, so we can't tell who sent it. Please don't report text that contains personal or sensitive information.

The report passes through our website host, Vercel, which sees your IP address in transit (we don't record it), and is stored as a row in a Google Sheets spreadsheet in our Google account (Google LLC, USA). Only we can open it. We keep reports to fix and improve reverse.ai. To have your report deleted, write to us with the words and roughly when you sent it.

5. What we don't do

No account or sign-up. No analytics or telemetry. No advertising. No selling or "sharing" of personal information, as those terms are defined in California law. No tracking across websites or apps. Because we don't track you, browser "Do Not Track" signals don't change anything.

6. Why we process this information

  • The update check and the website: our legitimate interest in delivering, maintaining and securing the app. You can turn the update check off.
  • Emails and log files you send: to handle your request.
  • Reports you send: your choice to send them, to fix and improve the app.
  • The permissions on your computer: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time in your system settings.

7. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect what we're responsible for: we build and publish the releases ourselves, the Mac app is code-signed, and the app checks for updates only against our own website. No system is perfectly secure.

The most important protection is built into the design: we don't hold your typing, so a breach of our accounts or website could not expose it. The realistic risk is a tampered copy of the app, so download reverse.ai only from reverseai.vercel.app or our official GitHub releases page. If we learn that our distribution has been compromised, we'll post a notice on the website and, where possible, through the app's update notice, and we'll notify affected users and authorities as the law requires.

  • United States: depending on your state (for example, California), you may have rights to know, delete and correct personal information and to opt out of its sale or sharing. We don't sell or share it, and we won't treat you differently for using your rights.
  • EU and UK: if the GDPR applies, you also have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability, and you may complain to your local data protection authority.

In practice we hold almost nothing about you. To use any of these rights, email kazakov100+reverseai@gmail.com.

9. Children

reverse.ai isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we'll delete it.

10. International transfers

Our service providers (Vercel, GitHub and Google) are based in the United States and may process request data there, under their own safeguards.

11. Changes to this policy

We'll post any change on this page with a new date. For important changes, we'll also say so on the website or in the app.
